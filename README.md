🌱 About STARK

STARK is an AI-powered farmer market intelligence and profit optimization platform developed for Smart India Hackathon 2026.

Farmers often have access to information such as market prices, crop details, weather conditions, expenses, transportation costs, and buyer offers. However, this information is often scattered across different sources.

STARK brings these factors together into one simple platform and helps farmers make better decisions about:

Where to sell

When to sell

At what price to sell

Which buyer to choose

Expected profit

Market selection

Agricultural product purchases


Our Core Idea

> Don't just show farmers information. Help them make better decisions from that information.




---

🎯 Vision

Our vision is to create a farmer-centric digital platform that simplifies agricultural decision-making through market intelligence and intelligent recommendations.

Instead of asking:

> "What is today's market price?"



STARK aims to answer:

> "Considering my crop, expenses, market, transportation, buyer offer, and spoilage risk — what should I do today?"




---

🚨 Problem We Address

Farmers face several challenges throughout the agricultural selling process.

💰 Profit Uncertainty

A high crop price does not always mean high profit because farmers also have production, transportation, storage, and other expenses.

📈 Market Price Fluctuation

Agricultural prices can change frequently, making it difficult to decide whether to sell immediately or wait.

🏪 Market Selection

Different markets may offer different prices, but transportation costs and market conditions affect the actual profit.

⚖️ Price Transparency

Farmers may receive buyer offers without knowing whether the offered price is fair.

🥬 Crop Spoilage

For perishable crops, waiting for a better price can result in spoilage and financial loss.

🛒 Input Purchasing

Farmers may find it difficult to identify suitable agricultural products for their specific crops and requirements.

⭐ Key Features

1. 💰 Profit Meter

The Profit Meter estimates the farmer's expected profit using:

Crop

Land area

Expected yield

Production cost

Market price

Transportation cost

Other expenses


Example

Expected Revenue       ₹1,00,000
Production Cost          ₹32,000
Transportation Cost       ₹3,000
Other Expenses            ₹2,000
--------------------------------
Expected Profit          ₹63,000

Benefit

Farmers can understand their expected net profit instead of looking only at the selling price.


---

2. 🔮 What-If Profit Simulator

The What-If Simulator allows farmers to test different scenarios before making a decision.

Farmers can change:

Crop price

Expected yield

Transportation cost

Production expenses

Selling conditions


Example

Current Profit
₹68,000

If price decreases by 10%

New Profit
₹56,000

Potential Impact
-₹12,000

Benefit

Farmers can understand the potential financial impact of different situations before taking action.


---

3. 📡 Market Radar

Market Radar compares different markets and identifies the most suitable option.

It considers:

Current market price

Transportation cost

Expected net profit

Market conditions

Demand

Market congestion


Example

Market A
₹25/kg
Expected Profit: ₹65,000

Market B
₹27/kg
Expected Profit: ₹69,000
🏆 Best Option

Market C
₹24/kg
Expected Profit: ₹62,000

STARK does not simply select the market with the highest price.

It focuses on the market with the best expected overall profitability.


---

4. 🛡️ Fair Price Guard

Fair Price Guard helps farmers evaluate buyer offers.

The system compares:

Buyer offer

Current market price

Nearby market prices

Reference price

Crop quality

Estimated fair price


Example

Buyer Offer
₹22/kg

STARK Estimated Fair Price
₹26/kg

Fair Price Score
58/100

⚠️ Offer is below the estimated fair price.

Suggested Negotiation Range
₹25–26/kg

Benefit

Farmers can make better-informed decisions while negotiating with buyers.


---

5. 🥬 Sell Now or Wait?

This feature helps farmers decide whether to sell immediately or wait for a potentially higher price.

STARK considers:

Current price

Expected future price

Crop quantity

Spoilage risk

Storage cost

Expected revenue


Example

SELL NOW

Price: ₹26/kg
Quantity: 4,000 kg
Revenue: ₹1,04,000


WAIT 5 DAYS

Price: ₹29/kg
Estimated Quantity: 3,400 kg
Revenue: ₹98,600


STARK Recommendation

🟢 SELL NOW

Why It Matters

A future price increase does not always mean higher profit.

For perishable crops, the quantity lost through spoilage can outweigh the benefit of waiting for a higher price.
